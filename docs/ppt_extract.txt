SATQUERY AI 
● Natural-language interface to query satellite and geospatial data, converting user queries into 
automated data selection and analysis workflows.
● Integrates satellite imagery, geospatial processing and AI/ML for analysis, delivering results 
through interactive maps, visualizations and concise insights.
● Eliminates the need for complex GIS/satellite-data expertise while automating data 
discovery, preprocessing and analysis.
● Reduces the time and effort required to extract insights, enabling researchers, officials and 
other users to access actionable satellite insights easily.
● Natural Language → Geospatial Analysis pipeline with AI-driven conversion of queries into 
relevant analytical workflows.
● Supports multi-source satellite/geospatial datasets and combines automated analysis, visual 
results and human-readable insights.
